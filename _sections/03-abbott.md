## Abbott client context

Abbott is a global healthcare and health-technology company. Its public portfolio spans cardiovascular care, diabetes, diagnostics, pain and movement, nutrition, medicines, and biowearables.

Even an internal technology project may connect to products, laboratories, manufacturing, supply chains, healthcare professionals, consumers, patients, or regulated business processes. Assess potential health, quality, privacy, operational, and reputational impact—not only technical complexity.

### Use the Abbott lens

- **Who could be affected?** Identify users, customers, patients, healthcare professionals, laboratory or manufacturing colleagues, and support teams.
- **What business area is involved?** Confirm the sponsoring business, products, processes, markets, sites, and systems.
- **What is the impact?** Consider patient safety, product quality, clinical decisions, manufacturing, supply continuity, privacy, and regulated records.
- **Which controls apply?** Confirm the applicable Abbott procedures and approval authorities with the client.
