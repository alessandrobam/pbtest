## Proposal lifecycle

1. **Qualify** — confirm opportunity and fit.
2. **Shape** — define scope, outcomes, and approach.
3. **Estimate** — model effort, cost, schedule, and risk.
4. **Propose** — prepare and approve the commercial package.
5. **Contract** — review, approve, and sign.
6. **Handoff** — transition the commitment into delivery.

Use this SOP to choose the contract path, estimate work, select a pricing method, establish the delivery baseline, and retain evidence through closure.
