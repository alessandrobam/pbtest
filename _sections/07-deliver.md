## Delivery lifecycle

### 1. Contract handoff

Review the executed agreement, scope, exclusions, assumptions, deliverables, acceptance, pricing, billing triggers, client obligations, dependencies, and open risks. The Delivery Lead explicitly accepts the baseline.

### 2. Mobilize

Confirm the team, skills, capacity, access, environments, repositories, governance forums, reporting cadence, escalation route, decision rights, and client participation.

### 3. Plan and baseline

Translate contractual deliverables into work packages with owners, estimates, dependencies, milestones, acceptance points, resource needs, financials, quality controls, and a measurable forecast.

### 4. Execute and govern

Track work against the approved baseline. Maintain the plan, RAID, decisions, changes, defects, financials, and evidence. Forecast early and escalate when agreed tolerances are crossed.

### 5. Validate, accept, and release

Trace results to approved requirements and acceptance criteria. Resolve critical defects, document residual risk, verify operational readiness, obtain release approval, and retain formal client acceptance.

### 6. Close and transition

Resolve or transfer every deliverable, invoice trigger, change, risk, and contractual obligation. Complete knowledge transfer, access removal, archiving, financial reconciliation, retrospective, and improvement actions.

## Minimum controls

| Control | Minimum practice | Typical cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery plan | Track baseline, actuals, dependencies, resources, milestones, and forecast. | Weekly |
| RAID and decisions | Record owners, dates, mitigation, impact, and overdue decisions. | Weekly and continuously |
| Financial control | Reconcile effort, cost, revenue, margin, billing, and unbilled work. | Weekly or monthly |
| Change control | Assess scope, cost, time, risk, quality, and contract impact before work begins. | As requested |
| Quality and acceptance | Retain review, test, defect, deviation, traceability, and approval evidence. | At each deliverable |
| Stakeholder status | Report outcomes, forecast, decisions, risks, changes, and support needed. | Weekly; steering monthly |
