## What is PlanBuild?

PlanBuild is the team’s end-to-end operating model for converting an Abbott client opportunity into a well-defined commitment and then delivering that commitment successfully. It connects two responsibilities: **building the right proposal** and **delivering what was promised**.

This SOP establishes the minimum practices, controls, evidence, and decision points expected across that lifecycle. Scale the detail according to project tier, complexity, risk, intended use, and contractual requirements.

| SOP element | Definition |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Create consistent, transparent, and commercially responsible outcomes. |
| Scope | Proposal shaping, estimation, contracting, mobilization, execution, acceptance, transition, and closure. |
| Audience | Project Managers, Proposal Leads, Delivery Leads, technical specialists, commercial partners, finance, quality, privacy, and security roles. |
| Owner | PlanBuild Operations. |
| Review cycle | At least annually and after material process change. |

> If a required step is not applicable, record why and who accepted the exception. Abbott contractual, quality, legal, regulatory, privacy, and security requirements take precedence when they impose a higher standard.
